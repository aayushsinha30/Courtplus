# Legal rules for the engine (sources: India Code, Supreme Court orders and NALSA SOP, cited inline)


1. Law as charged (IPC or BNS); min and max punishment from the Act text; BPR&D table as a lookup aid. Framed charges (with order date/page) override FIR sections; if not framed, flag it.
2. s.479 covers undertrials in all pending cases, including those registered before 1 Jul 2024 (SC, 23 Aug 2024). Not convict-appellants.
3. Death or life possible for any charged offence -> `OUTSIDE_SCREEN`.
4. Pending investigation, inquiry or trial in more than one offence or multiple cases, or any other warrant/hold -> `NEEDS_LAWYER_REVIEW`. s.479(2) is "subject to the third proviso": still raise `CEILING_URGENT_REVIEW` when custody reaches the maximum.
5. Unknown prior conviction, unresolved custody, an unverified framed charge, or a court continuation order -> review.
6. Never convicted -> one-third (bond); otherwise one-half (bail). Subtract only documented delay caused by the accused; show raw and adjusted custody.
7. Threshold reached -> `APPLY_NOW`: "the superintendent shall forthwith make an application in writing" (s.479(3)). The court may still continue detention after hearing the prosecutor with written reasons. Filing, decision and release are separate states.
8. Day counting: one demo convention printed on every trace; the advocate reviews it first; uncertain cases go to review.
9. **Default bail (s.187(3)) is a review alert only, never `APPLY_NOW`.** 90 days for death, life or a term of ten years or more; 60 otherwise. Any offence with maximum of 10 years or more, minimum under 10 years, and no death/life -> review, reason: "60 or 90 days is disputed for this offence: lawyer to check" (cite Hyder Ali, Karnataka HC 13 Dec 2024, and Mohammed Sajjid, Kerala HC 10 Feb 2025, once a teammate opens them). Inputs also include "ready to furnish bail: yes / no / unknown". The alert needs first remand date, expiry, chargesheet date and whether the accused applied before it was filed (*M. Ravindran*); special laws and old CrPC cases -> review.
10. Post-bail: 7 days from grant without release -> superintendent informs DLSA Secretary; 1 month from grant with bonds unfurnished -> granting court may relax conditions on its own (SC, 31 Jan 2023). Multiple pending cases can also explain non-release: check holds first. Routes for the lawyer: s.478 (bailable; indigence presumed after a week from **arrest**), s.484 (reduce bond), s.483 (HC/Sessions), Support to Poor Prisoners in two tiers (open the MHA revised SOP, Dec 2025, and quote it before shipping): hard exclusions (PC Act, PMLA, NDPS, UAPA) hide the referral suggestion; the discretionary heinous-offence list shows "committee discretion: lawyer review", never hidden. Never auto-waive.
11. NDPS, UAPA, PMLA, POCSO, juvenile claim (JJ s.94), mental illness (MHCA s.103) -> review tags.


## Additions from v5 reviews
12. Retrospective mode: screen each real order only with facts and law in force on its date (`asOf` = order date). BNSS s.479 applies from 1 Jul 2024 and to pending cases registered earlier (SC 23 Aug 2024); before 1 Jul 2024 use CrPC s.436A (one-half only, no first-time one-third) and mark it.
13. Silence in an order means `unknown`, never `no`.
14. Police custody (s.187(2)/(3) BNSS): total police custody in the same investigation must not exceed 15 days and must fall within the first 40 days (offences with 60-day limit) or 60 days (90-day limit) from first remand. Flag over 15 days and outside the window. CrPC cases -> review.
15. NALSA revised UTRC SOP (16 categories): 1 half-maximum (436A/479), 2 first-time one-third (479), 3 bail granted surety not furnished, 4 compoundable, 5 bailable (436/478), 6 probation candidates, 7 sentence completed/remission, 8 default bail incl. NDPS, 9 offence max two years, 10 preventive detention / security sections, 11 sick/infirm/terminally ill, 12 women, 13 first-time aged 18-21 offence below 7 years with one-quarter served, 14 unsound mind, 15 magistrate trial unfinished 60 days after first evidence (437(6)/480(6)), 16 aged 70+. Tag only what the facts support.
16. Antil categories (Satender Kumar Antil v CBI, 2022): A = punishable up to 7 years (not in B/D); B = death, life or more than 7 years; C = special Acts with twin conditions (NDPS s.37, PMLA s.45, UAPA s.43D(5), Companies Act s.212(6)); D = economic offences not covered by special Acts. Information tag only.
17. "Court and jail records disagree": a court event acquitted / bail_granted / released for a case while the person is still on the roster after the event date -> COURT_JAIL_MISMATCH alert ("verify order, identity and all current holds"). "No court update for 180 days": no court event in the last 180 days before asOf -> NO_COURT_UPDATE_180 (a check interval, not a legal deadline).
