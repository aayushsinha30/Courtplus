# CourtPlus engine

`screen`, `policeCustodyCheck`, `lookupOffence`, and `explain` are pure functions. The caller supplies the screening date. Statutory text and offence data are bundled locally.

```sh
npm install
npm run build -w @courtplus/contracts
npm run build -w @courtplus/engine
npm test -w @courtplus/engine
```

Edit [offences.csv](../../data/crosswalk/offences.csv) and [quotes.json](../../data/statutes/quotes.json) to update the sources. Run `npm run generate:data -w @courtplus/engine` and commit the regenerated TypeScript alongside the source. The build and test scripts check that the generated files match. Rule version `rules-2026-11-23.1` travels with each result.

## Civil days

Dates are Asia/Kolkata civil dates. Include the arrest or first custody day and the supplied `asOf` day when custody continues. A spell ending on a date excludes that date. An open spell must be marked `ongoing` or have a recorded release date. Overlapping police and judicial periods count once. Subtract interim bail to get raw detention, then subtract the intersection of documented accused delay and detention to get counted detention. The calendar maximum starts from the first actual custody day. Add years or months in one step, clamping to the target month's last day, and take the difference. For a fraction `1/k`, require `k × counted days ≥ maximum days`. A projected threshold assumes continuous countable custody after `asOf`. The trace prints the convention for advocate review.

## Detention review

1. Use the charged Act. A verified framed set replaces FIR sections. Use `EngineCharge.framedOn` for the date of the framing order, especially when several orders changed the charges, and include the source page where available. The FIR is provisional when framing is absent. An applicable unverified framed charge or unresolved penalty goes to lawyer review. Set `asOf` to the order date for retrospective screens. [Supreme Court order, 19 November 2024, para 11](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2013/18545/18545_2013_4_10_57306_Order_19-Nov-2024.pdf)
2. Before 1 July 2024, use the CrPC half-maximum rule. From 1 July 2024, apply the BNSS detention rule to pending undertrials, including earlier registered cases. Convict-appellants and disposed cases are outside the detention screen. Other checks can still flag an outstanding court/jail reconciliation. [CrPC s.436A and BNSS s.479](../../data/statutes/quotes.json), [Supreme Court order, 23 August 2024](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2013/18545/18545_2013_9_2_55018_Order_23-Aug-2024.pdf)
3. Death or life as an available punishment places the case outside the finite-maximum screen. Unknown prior conviction, unresolved custody, a continuation order, multiple pending offences or cases, and active or unknown holds route to review. The review still flags a verified finite maximum reached as `ceilingUrgent` and includes `CEILING_URGENT_REVIEW` in the reasons. For multiple finite charges, the trace uses the highest listed term as its comparison point and records each charge. [BNSS s.479(1)–(2)](../../data/statutes/quotes.json)
4. Under BNSS, use one-third for a person never convicted and half for a person previously convicted. `APPLY_NOW` under BNSS signals a written application under s.479(3). The court may order continued detention with written reasons after hearing the prosecutor. Filing, court decision, and actual release are separate events. [BNSS s.479](../../data/statutes/quotes.json)
5. Charges under NDPS, UAPA, PMLA, POCSO and the PC Act, a juvenile claim or a mental-health claim require specialist review. Use the verified charge and statutory procedure when these rules intersect. [JJ Act s.94](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_13_14_000010_201602_1517807328168&filename=a2016-2.pdf&type=actfile), [MHCA s.103](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_12_13_00024_201710_1517807327874&filename=a2017-10.pdf&type=actfile)

## Alerts and information tags

- Default-bail review includes remand day one. The first check after 60 or 90 full days is first remand plus 60 or 90 calendar days. Death, life and a minimum term of ten years or more select 90. A maximum of ten years or more with a shorter minimum is disputed. The trace shows applicable candidate dates, report and application chronology, and readiness. Civil dates leave same-date sequence open. CrPC savings and special-law periods and extensions go to counsel. [BNSS s.187(3)](../../data/statutes/quotes.json), [Kapil Wadhawan](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2020/17613/17613_2020_16_1501_43163_Order_27-Mar-2023.pdf), [M. Ravindran](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2020/8936/8936_2020_39_1501_24488_Judgement_26-Oct-2020.pdf), [Hyder Ali](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/122613847/), [Mohammed Sajjid](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/130785226/)
- Police custody unions police spells in the same investigation. It checks the 15-day total and the first 40 or 60 days from first remand. Missing dates, disputed classifications and CrPC procedure go to review. The standalone API uses `orderDate` or the last recorded custody or court day as its horizon. Supply `orderDate` to assess an ongoing police spell through a specific day. `withinWindow: false` can also mean the window could not be established. [BNSS s.187(2)–(3)](../../data/statutes/quotes.json)
- Section 478 uses a week from the verified arrest date for a bailable case where the bail bond remains unfurnished. Underlying classification can be supplied with the court record where a schedule entry is missing or locally amended. Sections 483 and 484 identify modification and reduction routes for counsel. [BNSS ss.478, 483 and 484](../../data/statutes/quotes.json)
- Seven days after bail grant without release, the superintendent informs the DLSA Secretary. At one calendar month from grant, if bail bonds remain unfurnished, the granting court may review conditions on its own motion. Check other holds. From 2 December 2025, screen a documented financial barrier under the revised Poor Prisoners SOP. The four named special Acts suppress referral. The separate heinous-offence tier stays visible for committee review. [Supreme Court directions, 31 January 2023](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2021/26546/26546_2021_2_23_41396_Order_31-Jan-2023.pdf), [Revised Poor Prisoners SOP, undertrial para 6](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ec05ba304f3809ed31d0ad97b5a2b5df/uploads/2025/12/2025121733.pdf#page=18)
- Acquittal, bail grant or release recorded before a positive roster observation or continued case custody can trigger the reconciliation check. Verify order, identity and holds. A record without a court update in 180 days triggers a status check. [Devilal Gond, 2026 INSC 1226](https://www.sci.gov.in/sci-get-pdf/?diary_no=354632026&from=latest_judgements_order&order_date=2026-11-16&type=j)
- NALSA tags cover these screening facts: 1 half maximum, 2 first-time third, 3 unfurnished surety, 4 compoundable, 5 bailable, 6 probation candidate, 7 completed sentence/remission, 8 default-bail review, 9 up to two years, 10 preventive detention, 11 sick/infirm, 12 women, 13 first-time age 18–21 with a maximum below seven years and at least a quarter served, 14 unsound mind, 15 non-bailable magistrate trial after 60 consecutive custody days from the first scheduled evidence date, 16 age 70 or older. [Revised NALSA categories, pp. 4–5](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s32e45f93088c7db59767efef516b306aa/uploads/2026/02/20260209543755368.pdf#page=4), [BNSS s.480(6)](../../data/statutes/quotes.json)
- Antil tags A, B, C and D describe up to seven years, death/life or above seven years, special Acts with applicable twin conditions, and other economic offences. The tag is informational. [Satender Kumar Antil, 11 July 2022](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2021/27955/27955_2021_5_1505_36261_Judgement_11-Jul-2022.pdf)

`explain(result)` returns an ordered array of status, alert, and lane keys with date or day-count parameters where applicable.

## Evidence and offence data

`EngineCaseRecord` and `EnginePerson` extend the shared shapes with optional sourced facts. A dated fact needs `on`, `value`, and a `source.docId`. Case evidence covers arrest, procedure, continuation orders, bail bonds, financial barriers, applicable classifications, and judicial milestones. Person evidence covers the jail roster, age and health. Supply case status and undated base flags as known at the screening date. The engine preserves silence as unknown. Callers that store only the base contracts should retain the optional evidence before enabling checks that depend on it.

The CSV records the statutory range and equivalent BNS provision where applicable. Finite terms are years or `Nm` for a term in months. Zero minimum means the provision sets no express minimum. Named variants distinguish provisions that cannot safely share a term. `lookupOffence.maxDays` is a reference estimate where the contract supplies no date. `screen.maxTermDays` uses the actual calendar anchor. Excise rows cover the Chhattisgarh Act. The snapshot reflects the cited editions and highlights substantive changes and ambiguity in the row notes. Supply `EngineCharge.offenceOn` when the offence date determines the applicable edition. Records that predate a listed version of the Arms, NDPS or Chhattisgarh row go to lawyer review where that version affects the listed penalty. Confirm local amendments when applicable. [BPR&D correspondence table](https://bprd.mha.gov.in/uploads/pdf/COMPARISON%20SUMMARY%20BNS%20to%20IPC%20.pdf), [India Code IPC](https://indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/4219/1/THE-INDIAN-PENAL-CODE-1860.pdf), [India Code BNS](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20062/1/a2023-45.pdf), [India Code Chhattisgarh Excise amendment](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/21461/1/the_chhattisgarh_excise_act%2C_1915_no._2_of_1915_date_10.04.1915.pdf)
